## Stable-curve diagrams

The schematic-curve and dual-graph material in the following locations includes adaptations of drawings and source code from *Reduction of Plane Quartics and Cayley Octads* by Raymond van Bommel, Jordan Docking, Vladimir Dokchitser, Reynald Lercier, and Elisa Lorenzo García:

- `docs/diagram-sources/genus-2-diagrams.tex`
- `docs/diagram-sources/genus-3-diagrams.tex`
- `public/diagrams/`

The paper is available at [arXiv:2309.17381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17381). Further adjustments to the schematic curves were made with [Hobby Editor](https://github.com/max-schwegele/hobby-editor).
